Six Stars Media

Seis Estrellas (ES) · Six Stars (EN)

Six Stars Media runs two YouTube channels that publish short, edited highlight clips of livestreamers playing video games, with original hooks, subtitles and visible credit to every streamer.

We use an internal, non-public command-line tool to upload these Shorts to our own two channels and to read our own channels' performance. The tool has no external users.

Seis Estrellas Media publica clips cortos de streamers jugando videojuegos en sus propios canales de YouTube (español e inglés), con atribución visible a cada streamer.
